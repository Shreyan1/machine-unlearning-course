# Your certificate

Score 16 or more on the [final test](final.md) and you can make a certificate here: type your name, download the
image and share it. It lists the topics you covered and the badges you earned along the way.

<div class="cert" markdown></div>

## About this certificate

It is a fun record of finishing a free course, not a qualification. Nobody grades or signs it: your browser makes it
from the quiz scores saved on this device, and you type the name yourself. That is why it claims no awarding body,
board or accreditation, only what you did, which was pass the final test.

**Your name stays on your device.** It is drawn into the image and remembered in this browser so you do not have to type
it again. It is not sent anywhere unless you choose to share the image or join the Hall of Finishers.

## The Hall of Finishers

Want your name on the site? Press **Join the Hall of Finishers**. It opens a GitHub issue with your details filled in;
attach your certificate image and submit it. Once the request is checked, your name is added to the
[Hall of Finishers](finishers.md). Requests come from a GitHub account, so each one can be traced to a real person.
