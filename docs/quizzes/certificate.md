# Your certificate

Pass every page quiz and score 16 or more on the [final test](final.md), and you can make a certificate here. First read and
accept the [terms of this certificate](#terms-of-this-certificate), then type your name, download the image and share it. It lists the topics you covered and the badges you earned along the way.

<div class="cert" markdown></div>

## About this certificate

It is a fun record of finishing a free course, not a qualification. Nobody grades or signs it. Your browser makes it
from the quiz scores saved on this device, and you type the name yourself. That is why it claims no awarding body,
board or accreditation, only what you did, which was pass every page quiz and the final test.

**A certificate is personal.** Once you download or share it, the name is locked in this browser. You can still change
it, for example to fix a typo, but that gives the certificate a new ID, so earlier copies no longer match.

**Anyone can make one, so the ID is what counts.** Because there are no accounts, nothing stops someone from typing a
different name. That is why each certificate prints an ID, and why only certificates listed in the
[Hall of Finishers](finishers.md#verify-a-certificate), checked by a person, count as verified.

**Your name stays on your device.** It is drawn into the image and remembered in this browser so you do not have to type
it again. It is not sent anywhere unless you choose to share the image or join the Hall of Finishers.

## The Hall of Finishers

Want your name on the site? Press **Join the Hall of Finishers**. It opens a GitHub issue with your details filled in;
attach your certificate image and submit it. Once the request is checked, your name is added to the
[Hall of Finishers](finishers.md). Requests come from a GitHub account, so each one can be traced to a real person.
