# Your progress

Pass a page's quiz (at least 60% right, every question answered) and the page counts as done. Your progress is saved
**in this browser only**, like your quiz scores, and nothing is sent anywhere. See
[Privacy and your data](../privacy.md) for exactly what is kept and how to delete it.

<div class="psum" markdown></div>

## Badges

Eleven badges, one for each milestone. Once you earn a badge it stays, even if you retry a quiz later. A day counts
towards your weekly streak when you answer a quiz, pick a scenario or mark a practice problem as done; missing a week
just starts a new streak.

<div class="badge-shelf" markdown></div>

## Your climb

The [Ladder of Forgetting](../unlearning/ladder-of-forgetting.md) is the map of the course. Its rungs are grouped
into five levels, one for each family of models, and you climb a level by passing the quizzes on the pages that teach it.

## Keep a copy

Your progress is kept in this browser only. Some browsers clear it after a few weeks without a visit, and it does not
follow you to another device. Save it to a file now and then, and load the file here to get everything back. The file
stays on your device.
