# Quizzes

Every page on this site ends with a short quiz. This page lists them all, with your score so far. Scores are saved **in
this browser only**; nothing is sent anywhere. To clear a quiz, open its page and press **Try again**.

When you have finished a section, try the [final test](final.md), 20 mixed questions from across the course.
